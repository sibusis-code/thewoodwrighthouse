Delivery, warranty and privacy

The plain-language terms that apply when you order from us.

These terms are a working draft. They are written to be fair and to sit alongside the Consumer Protection Act, but they have not yet been checked by a legal professional and they are not legal advice. Please have them reviewed before you rely on them.

Quotes and prices

Prices on this website are estimates. We confirm your final quote in writing, and the price is held for 14 days from the date of the quote. If the cost of materials moves sharply before you accept, we will tell you before we start rather than change the price afterwards.

Deposits and payment

Each piece is made to order. Once you agree your final quote, we ask for a % deposit to start the build. The balance is due before delivery. Please use your payment reference so we can match your payment.

Changes and cancellations

Tell us as early as you can. You may change or cancel your order at no cost up until the workshop starts cutting, and we will refund your deposit in full. Once work has started the piece is being made to your size, fabric and finish and cannot be sold to anyone else, so the deposit is no longer refundable. If you want to change the fabric or finish after work has started, we will tell you what it costs before we do anything.

Delivery

We deliver to . Delivery is charged once per order and shown in your quote. Please make sure someone over 18 is home to receive your order, and measure your doorways, passages, stairs and lifts before you order. We carry the piece in and place it where you want it, and we assemble anything that needs assembling, usually legs. We are responsible for damage in transit, so check your piece before you sign for it and tell us straight away if anything is wrong. If nobody is home at the agreed time, or the piece cannot fit into the room, we will bring it back and a second delivery is charged again.

Warranty

The frame of every piece is covered by a warranty of against defects in materials and workmanship. Fabric, foam, stitching and zips are covered for six months against faults in the material or the making. The warranty does not cover normal wear, fading in direct sunlight, spills, stains, pets, damage from misuse or cleaning, or any piece that has been altered or repaired by someone else. It covers the person who bought the piece and does not transfer if you sell it on. None of this affects your rights under the Consumer Protection Act.

Damaged or faulty items

Please check your piece when it arrives and tell us straight away about any damage or fault, and within seven days at the latest. Send us photographs and we will arrange to repair it, replace it, or refund you. We cover the cost of collection and return on anything that is genuinely faulty. Because every piece is made to your own specification, we cannot take a piece back simply because you have changed your mind, so please order swatches and check your measurements before you commit.

Your personal information

We collect the details you give us, such as your name, phone number, email and delivery address, only to give you a quote, make and deliver your order, and answer your questions. We do not sell your information. We share it only with the workshop and delivery partners who need it to fulfil your order. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time by contacting us at . We keep your order records for five years, as the tax rules require, and we keep enquiries that do not become orders for twelve months. We do not send marketing unless you ask us to. Our Information Officer under POPIA can be reached at the same address, and if you are not happy with how we have handled your information you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.
