How it works

We work with skilled local workshops, and we look after everything between your idea and your front door.

1

Choose your piece

Use the shop to pick a style and size, and see it in 3D. Add it to your quote. You can add more than one piece.

2

Pick fabric and finish

Choose a fabric and leg finish. Order swatches first if you want to see them in your own light.

3

Confirm and pay a deposit

We send your final quote in writing. Once you agree, you pay a % deposit and the workshop starts.

4

Built, checked, delivered

We check the finished piece before it leaves the workshop, then deliver it. The balance is due before delivery.
